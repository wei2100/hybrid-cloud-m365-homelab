# [SOP-001] End-User Guide: How to Book Meeting Rooms & Company Equipment via Outlook

**Target Audience:** All Employees  
**Maintained By:** IT Helpdesk  

## 1. Overview
To prevent scheduling conflicts for shared company resources, IT has configured Resource Mailboxes in Exchange Online. Employees can check real-time availability and reserve the following assets directly through Outlook:
* **Meeting Rooms:** Conference Room 01 (`Room-Meeting01@...`)
* **Company Equipment:** Company Vehicle (`Equip-Car01@...`), Marketing DSLR Camera (`Equip-Camera01@...`)

## 2. Step-by-Step Booking Instructions
1. Open **Outlook** and switch to the **Calendar** view on the left navigation bar.
2. Click **New Event** (or **New Meeting**) at the top left.
3. Enter a clear meeting title (e.g., `Client Visit Vehicle Booking - John Doe`) and select your desired date and time.
4. In the **Location** field (or **Invite attendees** field), type the resource name (e.g., `Equip-Camera01`) and select it from the directory.
5. Click **Scheduling Assistant** at the top to verify that the resource is available (no blue blocks indicating existing bookings) during your requested time slot.
6. Click **Send** to submit your reservation.

## 3. Confirmation & Conflict Handling
* **Approved:** If the slot is free, you will receive an automated email confirmation with the subject **"Accepted"** within 1 minute.
* **Declined:** If the resource is already booked by another colleague, the system will automatically send a **"Declined"** notification. Please adjust your time slot and resubmit.
